Built from a hundred square feet and a refusal to cut corners.

A letter from our director.

A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. In 1995 that step was a 100 sq. ft. workshop in Rajkot, a handful of tools, and a conviction that a flour mill built honestly would outlast one built cheaply.

We chose early on to make our machines ourselves rather than assemble someone else's parts. It was the slower path. It meant investing in tooling before we could afford it, and in people who cared whether a weld held. But it is the reason a Jagruti mill bought fifteen years ago is still grinding today, and the reason we can answer for every component inside one.

What started in that small unit has today grown into Jagruti Appliances, operating from a modern 35,000 sq. ft. ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing facility and serving families and businesses across India. The floor is larger and the machines are more precise, but what we check before a unit leaves has not changed.

Today we are proud to bring Jagruti into your kitchen.

Mr. Yash KoratDirector, Jagruti Appliances LLP

Jagruti Appliances LLP is operated under the supervision of Managing Director Mr. Mansukh Korat.

Built in-house, end to end.

Nothing leaves the Iscon Industrial Area facility that we did not cut, machine, assemble and test ourselves. Owning the whole process is slower than sourcing finished sub-assemblies, and it is the only way we can stand behind a machine for its full working life.

Total quality management runs at every stage of production under ISO 9001:2015 — not as a certificate on a wall, but as the checks a unit has to pass before it is boxed.
