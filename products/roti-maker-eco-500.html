Jagruti ECO 500 Automatic Roti Making Machine

500 Roti / Hour — Model ECO 500

Production Capacity: 500 Nos. / HR

A higher-capacity roti making machine producing 500 rotis an hour, with a wider adjustable chapati size and thickness range for growing food businesses.

  • Advance Three Layer Technology
  • Roti Baking Conveyor in SS 304
  • 360° Uniform Chapati Baking
  • Heating Element 1500 to 2500 Watts
  • Gas Safety — Auto Cut-Off Valve System
  • Machine Movable on Castor Wheels
