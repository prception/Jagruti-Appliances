FAQ

Common Questions

Which flour mill is best for home use?
It depends on your household's daily flour needs. Jagruti's Aura Plus is the flagship domestic model with automatic cleaning and digital controls, while Supreme and Kerona suit lighter everyday use. Message us on WhatsApp with your family size and we'll recommend the right model.
What grains can be used in a Jagruti domestic flour mill?
Jagruti domestic flour mills are designed for wheat, and most models also handle grains and pulses such as rice, maize, jowar, bajra and dal, depending on the model. Confirm grain compatibility for your chosen model on WhatsApp.
How much flour can a domestic flour mill produce?
Output varies by model. Share your daily flour requirement over WhatsApp and our team will match it to the right Jagruti model and confirm current capacity.
How much power does a domestic flour mill consume?
Domestic flour mills run on standard single-phase home electricity supply. Exact power ratings differ by model — ask us for the specification sheet of the model you're considering.
How should a domestic flour mill be maintained?
Regular cleaning of the grinding chamber, periodic checks of the chamber lining, and keeping the machine free of moisture will keep it running reliably for years. See our Maintenance guide in Support for details.

Tell our team what you need and we'll recommend the right Jagruti model for your home or business.
