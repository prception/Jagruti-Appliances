Maintenance

How to Maintain Your Machine

  • Clean the grinding chamber, blades or jars after every use to prevent build-up.
  • Check that the machine is dry and moisture-free before storage.
  • Keep ventilation slots and motor housings free of flour dust and debris.
  • Have gas connections, valves and pressure settings checked periodically on roti making machines.
  • Use only genuine Jagruti spare parts and blades to protect motor performance.
  • Schedule a professional service check at least once a year for commercial machines.
